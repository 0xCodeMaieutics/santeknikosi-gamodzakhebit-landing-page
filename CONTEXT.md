# სანტექნიკოსი გამოძახებით

A Tbilisi plumbing call-out business. The public site exists so a household with a plumbing problem can reach the plumber immediately.

## Language

**სანტექნიკოსი გამოძახებით**:
The business. Also written `santeqnikosi gamodzaxebit` in Latin script.
_Avoid_: Santservice as the public name (that is the informal / social handle, not the company name)

**Household**:
The person who needs plumbing work done and visits the site or Facebook page.
_Avoid_: Customer, client, user, lead

**Call**:
A voice conversation with the plumber on +995 599 45 70 70. This is the primary conversion.
_Avoid_: Lead form, contact form as the main ask

**WhatsApp Chat**:
A WhatsApp conversation with the same number, +995 599 45 70 70. Used when the Household wants to send photos or write instead of calling.
_Avoid_: SMS, Messenger as a documented channel unless we add it later

**Email**:
`santservice64@gmail.com`. A footer contact only, not a hero action.
_Avoid_: Email as a primary conversion, a contact form that mails this address

**Facebook page**:
The public page at profile id 61552343170403. A footer proof/social link, not a hero action.
_Avoid_: Embedding a live Facebook feed on v1

**Instagram**:
The public profile `sant.service`. A discovery and proof channel, not the primary conversion.
_Avoid_: Treating Instagram DMs as equal to Call

**თბილისი და შემოგარენი**:
The area a Household can get a visit. Not a nationwide service.
_Avoid_: All Georgia, named cities outside Tbilisi, a district-by-district list on v1

**Wordmark**:
The public mark is the Georgian name სანტექნიკოსი გამოძახებით, not a character mascot.
_Avoid_: Mario, Luigi, Nintendo art, TikTok-watermarked “logo” files

**ქართული**:
The language of all public copy on the site.
_Avoid_: English or Russian UI, a language switcher on v1

**24/7 გამოძახება**:
The plumber can be reached by Call or WhatsApp Chat at any hour.
_Avoid_: Weaker hours lines, “business hours”, implying a night answering service that is not this number

**Visit**:
The plumber arriving at the Household after a Call or WhatsApp Chat.

**Quote**:
The price for that job, given before work starts. The Visit itself starts at 50 ლარიდან.
_Avoid_: A published menu of Service prices

**ოჯახის სანტექნიკი გამოძახებით**:
The offer: a plumber who comes to the Household.
_Avoid_: Shop, store, showroom

## Services

**Service**:
Work the plumber will do after a Call or WhatsApp Chat. The public catalog is the seven items on the family-plumber card.

**კანალიზაციის წმენდა**:
Clearing a blocked drain or sewer.

**ონკანის დაყენება**:
Fitting a tap / faucet.

**უნიტაზის მონტაჟი**:
Installing a toilet.

**დუშკაბინის მონტაჟი**:
Installing a shower cabin.

**წყლის მილების დაბერვა**:
Water-pipe work that covers both laying/chasing the line and inspecting it with a camera (ნახვრეტით). Published as one Service, two kinds of job.
_Avoid_: დაბერება, დაბურვა; splitting camera inspection into a separate catalog item unless the card changes

**წყლის მილების შედუღება**:
Welding / joining water pipes.

**50 ლარიდან**:
The minimum Visit. Work on top is quoted before it starts.
_Avoid_: 20 ლარიდან, seven Service prices, “free call-out”

**ყიდვა — თქვენზე | მონტაჟი — ჩვენზე**:
A promise on installation jobs: the Household supplies the fixture; the plumber installs it.
_Avoid_: Shop, cart, product catalog, selling appliances on this site

**ყველაფერი რაც სანტექნიკას შეეხება**:
Catch-all: any other plumbing job, not a separate product.
